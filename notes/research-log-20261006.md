# Campaign research log — architecture-20261006 (access date for all sources: 2026-10-06)

Independent literature search performed on the controller before any candidate screen, per `program.md` ("Autonomous literature search"). Three decision questions were investigated from Terminus measurements; full per-source verification status (VERIFIED full text vs abstract/snippet) was recorded by the searchers. This log condenses the trail; trial notes carry the per-candidate subset.

## Q1. Architecture-side mechanisms for ternary conversion shock and QAT sensitivity

Terminus measurements prompting the search: one-shot dense→ternary conversion of the 150M pilot: 52.9% relative weight reconstruction error, 31.7% zero codes, held-out 2.42→10.5 (recovered over ~200M QAT tokens); K projection worst per layer (error ≤0.83, zeros ≤44.8%); QAT latent weights drift away from ternary-friendly distributions while loss recovers; FP8 activation error benign (2.4% median rel-RMS).

- QuaRot 2404.00456 (VERIFIED full text; code Apache-2.0): randomized-Hadamard conjugation via SliceGPT computational invariance; ≤0.47 WikiText2 PPL loss at Llama-2-70B W4A4KV4; rotation helps MORE at 2–3 bits than 4 (App. A.4); Hadamard ≫ random orthogonal (App. A.5); benefit shrinks with width. No ternary measurement.
- SpinQuant 2405.16406 (VERIFIED; code **CC-BY-NC-4.0 — concepts only, no code reuse**): learned Cayley-Stiefel rotations; W4A4KV4 LLaMA-2-7B gap 2.9 pts; random rotations vary up to 13 pts across seeds (Fig. 4).
- DuQuant 2406.01721 (VERIFIED; MIT): Remark 1 — SmoothQuant-style smoothing pushes outliers into down-proj weights, worsening weight quantization. SmoothQuant 2211.10438 (MIT) therefore rejected for us: our FP8 activations are benign and the weight side is our bottleneck.
- ParetoQ 2502.02631 (VERIFIED local PDF; BSD): below 3 bits QAT is reconstruction (≈40% L1 weight change) not compensation — explains the pilot's latent drift as expected STE behavior, not a bug; optimal split ≈ 90% dense + 10% QAT; ternary saturates only ~30B QAT tokens; one-shot RTN ternary collapse (PPL 4×10^5 at 125M) vs our FP8-pretrained 2.42→10.5 — FP8 pretraining is already much better behaved.
- Continual QAT 2502.11895 (VERIFIED local PDF): earlier dense→QAT transition better; spike recovers in ~100 steps; optimizer-state carry-over damps spike but not endpoint; quant-strength phasing no lasting benefit.
- BitNet family (2310.11453, 2402.17764, 2411.04965, 2504.18415; all VERIFIED; code MIT): BitNet v2 H-BitLinear — online Hadamard on ACTIVATION inputs to o_proj/down_proj (outlier-heavy intermediate states; QKV/gate/up inputs already Gaussian-like); W1.58A4 diverges without it; activation-only ≈ weight+activation rotation. **Table 4 negative: fusing rotation into ternary-trained latent weights HURTS (PPL 19.8–20.8 vs 13.52 unfused)** — rotate-from-dense-start is untested in either direction.
- K-fragility: KIVI 2402.02750 (MIT) + KVQuant 2401.18079 — keys carry channel-wise outliers (consistent with our K-projection ternary fragility); their fixes are quantizer/granularity changes = out of scope.
- Scaling Laws for Precision 2411.04330 (VERIFIED): PTQ degradation grows as power law in tokens/params — converting a fully-converged dense model is the worst point; supports conversion-timing recommendation (stage-two/scheduling, not this campaign's yardstick).
- PeRQ 2601.22347 (abstract): block rotations fundamentally limited by block geometry — argues for full-hidden-dim rotation if ever done.
- **Conclusion:** the quantization-sensitive candidate family is activation-side fixed-Hadamard rotation at o_proj/down_proj inputs from dense start (trial-004, deferred — see below); conversion timing/phasing recommendations documented for stage two. No verified ternary-specific architecture fix exists in the literature; this is a genuine gap.

## Q2. Attention pathologies: layer-0 suppression, mid-layer low entropy, logit scale, residual migration

Terminus measurements: layer-0 attention gain 1.0→0.0165 with output RMS 0.076 vs FFN 3.18; 3.6% of sampled attention min-entropy <0.1 concentrated at layers 13/14; pilot max logit 54 (no QK norm); older affine-gain run hit logit 1515; residual RMS 0.61→191→34, QAT re-growth 34→48 with last-layer attention amplification 3.09→20.76.

- Peri-LN 2502.02732 (VERIFIED full text): learnable-output-norm per sublayer; 400M/1.5B/3.2B × 30B tokens × 5 seeds: best loss, no grad spikes, balanced hidden variance; Pre-LN top-100 activations exceed FP16 max from 0.5B tokens, Peri-LN never (Fig. 11); frozen γ removes benefit (Fig. 39). → trial-001.
- OLMo 2 2501.00656 (VERIFIED): post-sublayer RMSNorm + QK-norm; grad-spike 0.108→0.069; 7B–32B. Gemma-2 2408.00118 (VERIFIED): pre+post sublayer norms at production scale.
- Gated attention 2505.06708 (VERIFIED): −0.2 PPL, +2 MMLU at 1.7B/3.5T; sink share 46.7→4.8%; spike-free at high LR. Counter: SinkProbe 2609.08574 (abstract, Sep 2026) failed to reproduce the sink effect at small scale; 2606.08105 (abstract): gating relocates rather than removes sink computation. → trial-003 with predeclared falsifiers.
- Value residual 2410.17897 (VERIFIED full text): equal loss at −16% params/−20% tokens (82M–1.6B, 20B tokens); only V_1-sourced skips help; mitigates sinks/value drains/residual peaks (their Fig. 13). modded-nanogpt uses a variant (repo record). → trial-002.
- Layer-removal studies (2403.17887, 2406.19384, 2406.15786 — all VERIFIED): early layers are the MOST sensitive; deep/middle attention most prunable → layer-0 attention is not naturally dispensable; the pilot's collapse is an optimization pathology, not an architecture verdict.
- Kimi K2 MuonClip 2507.20534 (VERIFIED): Muon-specific attention-logit explosions; QK-clip τ=100 fixed it with no quality cost — but QK-clip is a training-loop/optimizer mechanism (protected code here); recorded as a new-campaign-version item if logits blow up under this campaign's Muon.
- Softcap (Gemma-2) → superseded by QK-norm (Gemma-3 2503.19786 §2.1, VERIFIED): not proposed. Wortsman 2309.14322 (VERIFIED): qk-LN cuts LR sensitivity 3 orders of magnitude at small scale. σReparam 2303.06296: spectral reparam; heavier machinery, not taken.
- Mix-LN 2412.13795 / DeepNorm 2203.00555 (VERIFIED): instability ≥1B in third-party runs — rejected. nGPT 2410.01131: forced unit-norm hidden states = excluded class — rejected. LNS 2502.05795 (Curse of Depth): zero-param 1/√depth LN scaling; partially redundant with Peri-LN — held as fallback.
- Differential attention 2410.05258 (VERIFIED): needs custom kernels under FA3/local-window — stage three.

## Q3. Parameter allocation: embedding fraction, depth/width, heads, window/cadence, μP transfer

- Tied embedding kept (Press & Wolf 1608.05859; Inan 1611.01462; MobileLLM 2402.14905 §2.2.3: tying −0.2 pts for −11.8% params at 125M). ALBERT-factorized TIED head rejected: softmax-bottleneck rank cap (1711.03953); ALBERT (1909.11942, VERIFIED) only clears input-side factorization.
- Vocab-law 2407.13623 (VERIFIED full text): for the 2.5B-token English screen, V_opt ≈ 15K — the fixed 65K multilingual tokenizer is a deliberate over-investment (~25% of params); screen losses carry a "vocab tax" equally for all candidates; do not read cross-domain conclusions from it.
- Depth/width conflict: MobileLLM (VERIFIED, 19 models ≤350M): deep-thin wins downstream zero-shot; Densing-law 2412.04315 (VERIFIED local PDF Table 1) + Kaplan neutrality + vocab-law fits: loss-neutral to aspect ratio at L/d ≈ 0.023–0.033. Resolution for this campaign: scorer is loss-based at stage one; geometry probes cost the same GPU as mechanism probes but address no measured Terminus pathology → deferred to the 1.5B shape search (stage two), with MobileLLM-vs-Densing conflict documented.
- GQA 2305.13245 (VERIFIED): quality evidence is uptraining-based T5; from-scratch MQA instability (App. A) warns against KV-head reduction at 200M. Head_dim 64 vs 128: no isolated published quality differential found (genuine gap); industry poles 64 (OpenBMB) / 128 (Llama/Qwen) / 256 (Gemma-3).
- Hybrid local/global: Gemma-3 2503.19786 (VERIFIED §5.2): 5:1 local:global with window 1024 (= our exact baseline), ratio pushable to 7:1 and window shrinkable with minimal perplexity impact — but perplexity-only ablations; retrieval-across-window is the documented weak class (Lost-in-the-Middle 2307.03172). Stage-one screen cannot see this (loss-only) → flagged as finalist transfer check.
- μP/Muon transfer: tensor-programs 2203.03466 (VERIFIED local PDF): pre-LN depth transfer OK; init std does NOT transfer across depth (recompute residual_init_scale ≈ 1/√(2L) if depth changes); regularization (wd) does not transfer — retune at 1.5B. muon_scalable 2502.16982 (VERIFIED local PDF): per-matrix √max(fan_in,fan_out) rule (= our sqrt_fan_in for fan_in ≥ fan_out) auto-rescales LRs across width/ffn/head changes; MiniCPM4 2506.07900 Table 3: μP transfer ≈ StepLaw at 32 GPU-hours.

## Deferred candidate: trial-004 (Hadamard-rotated intermediate states)

Mechanism (from Q1): fixed orthonormal transform (Kronecker fast Walsh–Hadamard, H_256⊗Helmert_3 for dim 768; H_128⊗Helmert_15 for ffn 1920; fixed-seed Rademacher signs) applied to o_proj and down_proj INPUTS from dense step 0. Weights learn in the rotated basis; prediction: lower FP8 activation error on those GEMMs during dense training (measurable at screen) and lower ternary conversion error at the QAT probe. Dense-quality expectation: neutral-to-slightly-better. Evidence: BitNet v2 (diverges without it at W1.58A4), QuaRot (rotation helps more at lower bits). Risks: BitNet-v2 Table 4 ternary-fusion negative (different direction), rotation-seed variance (SpinQuant Fig. 4), +2 serial transforms/layer (FWHT ≈ O(D log D), <2% FLOPs as butterflies; reference matmul path for parity tests only). **Deferred because the 12 VM-hour budget cannot fit its screen + full + matched QAT-probe pair (≈6.7h) alongside the pathology-targeted candidates; it is the top recommendation for the next campaign version if the probe budget exists.** Revisit immediately if a screen slot frees up.
