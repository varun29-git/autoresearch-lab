## How to research during the campaign

Start from a concrete measured question, e.g. “Why is this wgrad shape at 25% of its operand-inclusive roofline?” Search primary papers and official implementations for that shape/operation/hardware regime, a competing method and a negative result. Record query/date, section or code path, full revision, license and hardware/model/precision conditions. Read enough code/equations to establish whether the claimed method preserves this stack's contract. Paper abstract speedups or third-party summaries alone are not sufficient for an expensive implementation.

After a surprising gain or failure, research its mechanism again: changed rounding, activation liveness, matrix orientation, split-K reduction, occupancy, asynchronous dependency or update normalization. A source can falsify an idea before any GPU spend. Original execution ideas may proceed with a derivation and a cheap diagnostic without claiming they are unprecedented.

Comparators serve two roles: a stronger existing kernel can expose a poor local implementation; the physics model can expose slack remaining even after matching that comparator. Neither public leaderboard practice nor a vendor peak is the objective. Preserve every distinction among published measurement, local archived measurement, fresh native measurement, derivation and hypothesis.
